# Product Context & Business Blueprint

## Product Overview
Sportify Society is the official sports society of IIT Madras BS Degree program, promoting physical wellness, competitive sports tournaments, e-sports, watch parties, newsletters ("The Podium"), and community meetups nationwide.

## Council Structure
The core council consists of elected student representatives leading various operations:
- Secretaries / Executive Leadership
- Events & Operations
- Women's Wing (Sportify Her)
- PR & Outreach
- Design & Media
- Tech & Analytics
